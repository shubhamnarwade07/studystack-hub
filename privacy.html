Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026. Last updated: September 2026.

Notice regarding draft status: This privacy summary reflects the real architectural data handling of StudyStack Hub as a static, client-side educational directory. Legal review is advised prior to formal organizational publication.

1. Principle of Data Minimization

StudyStack Hub is designed from the ground up to respect student privacy. You can browse all directory entries, read framework guides, and search tools without registering an account, providing an email address, or logging in.

2. Browser Local Storage

When you save resources using the "Save to Stack" feature, the item identifier, title, and link are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage.getItem('studystack_saved_items')).

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any analytics partner. You can clear this saved data anytime by clearing your browser cache or using the removal buttons in the My Stack drawer.

3. Cookies and Tracking

StudyStack Hub does not use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Optional analytics tags (such as Google Analytics 4) are currently commented out as site templates and are not active by default.

4. Third-Party Web Links

Our directory contains direct links to independent third-party software websites (such as Zotero, Anki, or Notion). When navigating to external domains, your interactions are governed by the privacy policies and practices of those external sites.

5. Privacy Questions

If you have questions regarding data handling on this site, contact privacy@studystackhub.example.
